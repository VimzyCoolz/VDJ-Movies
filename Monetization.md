💰 VDJ Movies Monetization Strategy

The strategy has two sides: viewer revenue + creator rewards.

1. Free users — Ads

🎬 VDJ Movies — Free User Monetization

Free users can watch movies without ads interrupting playback.

At the Watch button before entering the landscape/fullscreen player, the button can appear as 🎁 Watch.

Tapping 🎁 Watch means the user voluntarily watches a 3 rewarded ad.

Completing that ad gives the user 45 minutes of ad-free viewing access.

During those 45 minutes, the user can freely watch any number of movies without another ad.

The 45-minute period is simply the duration of the ad-free reward window. It does NOT mean an ad will appear after 45 minutes.

While the reward is active, the Watch button shows ✅ Watch instead of 🎁.

Once the window has expired, the next time the user wants to start watching, 🎁 Watch can become available again.

VDJ controls when and where the 🎁 Watch option appears, rather than attaching a rewarded ad to every individual movie.


Core idea: the user isn't watching an ad for a movie(even if that ad will be played when the user is opening a movie); they're watching an ad to earn a period of ad-free VDJ viewing. 😜

2. Premium — Ad-free subscriptions

Users can pay for an uninterrupted viewing experience.

The tentative pricing we discussed:

Premium period	Price

1 day	KSh 10
7 days	KSh 50
30 days	KSh 150
90 days	KSh 400
365 days	KSh 1,000


Premium users get no ads and a countdown showing how much subscription time remains.

These prices were examples, not necessarily final.


---

🎬 3. Creator/DJ Revenue Pool

This is the important part for attracting DJs.

Instead of promising something arbitrary like KSh X per 1,000 views, VDJ creates a monthly Creator Revenue Pool.

Monthly calculation

VDJ first calculates eligible revenue from things such as:

Advertising

Premium subscriptions

Applicable sponsorship revenue


Then:

50% → VDJ
50% → Creator Pool

VDJ's 50% covers things like:

Infrastructure

Development

Operations

Marketing

Business costs

Profit


The other 50% is distributed to creators.


---

🏆 4. Only the top 30% of creators qualify

Creators are ranked using qualified watch time, rather than simply raw views.

If there are 10,000 eligible creators(uploaders):

Top 30% = 3,000 creators

Those 3,000 enter the Creator Pool calculation.

The remaining 7,000 don't receive that month's creator-pool payout.

Payout formula

> Creator payout = (Creator's qualified watch time ÷ Total qualified watch time of eligible creators) × Creator Pool



So someone doesn't need millions of views to potentially earn.

If they're inside the qualifying top 30% and have some qualified watch time, they receive their proportional share.


---

⏱️ 5. Qualified watch time is the core metric

VDJ already has its per-user view system, where a view isn't simply counted every time someone presses play. A substantial portion of the movie must be watched.

For monetization, however, watch time is more useful than raw views, especially because VDJ is focused on long-form DJ Movies.

So:

Views → popularity/discovery metric
Qualified watch time → creator monetization metric

This also makes gaming the system harder.


---

🚫 6. A movie can earn only once

This is one of the internal rules we discussed.

Once a particular movie earns a Creator Pool payout, that specific movie is permanently excluded from future Creator Pool rankings.

The creator is not permanently disqualified.

For example:

> DJ A uploads Movie X → Movie X earns → Movie X can never earn again.



But:

> DJ A uploads Movie Y → Movie Y can potentially earn later.



This encourages creators to keep bringing fresh content rather than repeatedly relying on the same successful movie.

We planned to keep the exact internal mechanics of this system private rather than exposing the anti-gaming algorithm publicly.


---

📱 7. Social media becomes the acquisition funnel

You also had a strong marketing loop:

DJ Movie → best ~1-minute clip → TikTok / Instagram / Facebook / YouTube Shorts → CTA → VDJ Movies

So instead of advertising VDJ with generic promotional videos, the actual content becomes the advertisement.

Example:

> 🔥 Best part of a DJ Movie
“Watch the full DJ Movie on VDJ Movies — app or website.”



DJs can also publish their own highlights with a VDJ outro, meaning DJs effectively help market VDJ while promoting their own content.


---

The overall flywheel 🔄

Free users
→ watch movies
→ ads generate revenue

Premium users
→ pay for ad-free viewing
→ subscription revenue

Revenue
→ 50% VDJ
→ 50% Creator Pool

Creators
→ compete based on qualified watch time
→ top 30% receive proportional payouts

Creators earn
→ incentive to upload more quality content

More content
→ more social-media clips
→ more discovery
→ more VDJ users
→ more ad + Premium revenue

That's essentially the monetization model we arrived at: make VDJ attractive to viewers with free content, attractive to paying viewers with ad-free Premium, and attractive to DJs through a revenue-sharing system tied to actual audience engagement. 🚀
